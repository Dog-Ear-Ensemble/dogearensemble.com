Write stories and roleplay with AI characters, on a model running on your own device or an online one you choose. Characters, personas, and chats are plain files you own.

Coming soon: Android and Linux first

What it does

Characters and personas

Create and edit characters, and the personas you play as.

Import character cards (chara_card_v2, as JSON or PNG).

Search Chub for characters, when you ask.

Chats

Many chats per character, saved as you go.

Import JanitorAI chat archives and SillyTavern chats.

A trash, so a deleted chat or message isn't gone for good.

Models

Run GGUF models on your device, downloaded from Hugging Face.

Or use online models through OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible address, with your own key.

Token counts show what fits in the context window.

On your terms

Nothing leaves without you

Opening the app makes no network connection at all.

It connects only when you press a button that says where it goes, or turn on a setting that does.

No account, no sign-up, no ads, no trackers.

Your files

Every character, persona, and chat is a plain YAML file in your Documents folder.

Back them up, read them, or move them with any tool you like.

API keys are kept apart, in your device's secure storage.

Before you start

For adults (18+). The character sources and online model providers the app connects to require users to be 18 or older, and so does this app.

Experimental. The app is early, provided as is, and may change or break. Keep backups of anything you care about.

AI makes things up. Replies can be wrong or offensive. Nothing a character says is advice.

Online models cost money. Requests to an online provider are billed to your own key, by that provider.

Support
