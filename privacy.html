Privacy

In short: {{ site.title }} keeps your data on your device and sends nothing unless you ask it to.

The app

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for, and we run no server that holds your data.
  • No connection on launch. Opening the app makes no network connection.
  • No trackers. No analytics, ads, or crash reporting.
  • Your data stays in files. Characters, personas, and chats are YAML files in your Documents folder. API keys go in your device's secure storage, not in those files.

When the app connects

Only when you press a button that says where it connects, or turn on a setting worded the same way. Each one goes straight from your device to that service:

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Hugging FaceYou search for or download a model.Your search words, the model you pick.
ChubYou search for or download a character.Your search words, the character you pick.
OpenRouter, or an address you enterYou chat using an online model.The chat, with the character and instructions the model needs to reply, and your API key.

Those services have their own privacy policies and terms, which apply to what you send them. A model running on your device sends nothing anywhere.

This website

No cookies, trackers, analytics, or third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement.
